# 1. Requirements (Clinic Appointments)

## Functional Requirements
- **R1:** A Patient shall be able to register an account and log into the system securely.
- **R2:** A Patient shall be able to view available doctor schedules and time slots.
- **R3:** A Patient shall be able to book an appointment with an available Doctor.
- **R4:** A Patient shall have a maximum of $N = 5$ active upcoming appointments at any given time.
- **R5:** A Doctor shall be able to view their daily list of scheduled appointments.
- **R6:** A Doctor shall be able to update the status of an appointment (e.g., Completed, Cancelled).

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **R7:** The system shall respond to user scheduling queries in under 2 seconds and encrypt all stored passwords using secure hashing algorithms.
